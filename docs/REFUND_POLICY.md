# Refunds and cancellations

> Decided Nov 11, 2026. This is the wording for the refund policy page on hexxsy.com. The theme's "Refund policy" pattern carries the same text, with the numbers read from the settings on WooCommerce > Vendors. If a number changes there, change it here too. Defaults: 24 hours to cancel, 7 days late before an automatic refund, 7 days after delivery to raise a problem.

Readings on Hexxsy are sold as entertainment. This is what happens when you want your money back.

## Written readings

**Changing your mind.** You can cancel a written reading for a full refund within 24 hours of paying, as long as it hasn't been delivered yet. Go to My readings in your account and press Cancel for a full refund. Once a reading has been delivered it can't be cancelled, even inside the 24 hours.

**After 24 hours.** Your reader has started work, so the order stands unless the reading comes late.

**Late readings.** Every reading shows a due date when you buy it. If yours hasn't been delivered by then:

- You and your reader both get an email.
- From that moment you can cancel for a full refund from My readings, or wait for the reading.
- If it still hasn't been delivered 7 days after the due date, Hexxsy cancels it and refunds you in full. You don't have to ask.

**Delivered readings.** A delivered reading isn't refunded. You have it, and it can't be handed back. Not liking what the cards said isn't grounds for a refund.

**When something is wrong.** If what you received isn't the reading you ordered, ignores your question entirely, or breaks the rules readers agree to, write to Hexxsy within 7 days of delivery. Hexxsy looks at each case and decides. A refund here may be full or partial.

**If your reader leaves Hexxsy.** If a reader's room closes before your reading is delivered, either they finish it by its due date or you're refunded in full. You'll be told by email which.

## Live calls

Live calls aren't open yet. When they are:

| Situation | You get |
| --- | --- |
| You cancel 24 hours or more before the call | Full refund |
| You cancel inside 24 hours | No refund |
| You don't show | No refund |
| Your reader cancels, at any time | Full refund |
| Your reader doesn't show | Full refund |

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, for the amount you paid. Hexxsy sends them straight away; banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show them.

## Notes for Hexxsy (not for the page)

- The contact address customers write to isn't decided. The pattern has a placeholder.
- Exceptions on delivered readings are made by hand: open the order in WooCommerce and use its Refund button. The reader's share shrinks with the refund. If the reader was already paid, WooCommerce > Payouts lists the amount to take back.
- A refund made inside the 7-day payout hold never reaches the reader. That's what the hold is for, and why the complaint window matches it.
- Whether a refunded customer's welcome code is reissued isn't decided. As built, the code is single-use and stays used.
