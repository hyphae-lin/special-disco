# Hexxsy reader code of conduct

> Copied from the working doc on claude.ai (Oct 13, 2026). That doc is where changes are discussed; this file is what the site builds from. Plain-language version; a formal version also exists in the working doc and needs a lawyer's review before readers sign it.

## Who this is for

Every reader who sells on Hexxsy agrees to this code. It sets the rules for being on the site and for how you treat the people who come to you.

It doesn't tell you how to read. Your deck, your spreads, your methods, your prices, your hours and your turnaround are yours. You're free to sell readings anywhere else too.

People often come to a reading carrying something heavy. This code is here so they leave no worse off than they arrived.

## Be honest about what a reading is

Readings on Hexxsy are sold for entertainment. Say nothing, in a listing or a reading, that promises otherwise.

- Don't guarantee outcomes. "Your ex will come back by spring" is a promise you can't keep.
- Describe your listings truthfully: what the customer gets, how long it takes, and how you work.
- Claim only the training, lineage or experience you actually have.
- Don't write or arrange fake reviews, for yourself or against another reader.

## Readings you don't give

Some questions belong with a doctor, lawyer or other professional. When a customer asks one, you can still read on how they feel about the situation and what they want from it. You don't predict the answer.

| Area | Don't | You can |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Health | Diagnose, predict illness or recovery, or suggest stopping or changing treatment | Read on how they're coping and what support they need |
| Pregnancy | Predict pregnancy, a baby's sex, or a birth outcome | Read on hopes, fears, readiness |
| Death | Predict when anyone will die | Read on grief, or on a relationship with someone who has died |
| Legal | Predict how a case or custody fight will turn out, or advise on one | Read on how they're carrying it |
| Money | Tell someone to buy, sell, gamble or invest | Read on their relationship with money and risk |
| Other people | Read on a third party to expose secrets, track them, or decide if they're cheating | Read on the customer's own side of the relationship |

If the question can't be read without crossing one of these lines, tell the customer plainly and point them to the right kind of help. Hexxsy will refund the order.

## No fear selling

Hard news can be part of a reading. Using fear to make a sale can't.

- Don't tell a customer they're cursed, hexed, haunted or under attack, and don't offer to remove it.
- Don't end a reading on a cliffhanger that only another purchase resolves.
- Don't push extra readings, spells, candles, crystals or services in a reading or a message.
- Don't create urgency: "you must act before the full moon" is pressure, not guidance.

You can mention your other offerings in your shop profile and listings. Customers find them there.

## When a customer is in crisis

You'll sometimes read a question from someone who is in real danger: talking about ending their life, being hurt by someone, or unable to keep themselves safe. A reading isn't the help they need right then.

- Don't read on whether they'll survive, whether to stay with someone who hurts them, or anything that could push them further in.
- Tell them kindly that you want them to talk to someone who can help now. In the US, that's the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988).
- Tell Hexxsy the same day, through the order, so we can follow up and refund the reading.

You don't have to be a counselor. You do have to notice and pass it on.

## Privacy and contact

Customers trust you with their questions, names and birth details. Keep that trust.

- Use what a customer shares only for their reading. Don't save it elsewhere, reuse it, or share it.
- Never post or describe a customer's reading in a way that could identify them, even as an example.
- Keep all contact with customers on Hexxsy. Don't ask for their email, phone or social accounts, and don't offer yours.
- Don't take payment outside Hexxsy for anything a customer found through the site.

## Respect and keeping your word

- Treat every customer with respect, whatever their race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, age or beliefs.
- No sexual content, flirting or romantic talk with customers.
- Write each reading yourself, for that customer and their question. No copy-and-paste readings, and no readings generated by AI and passed off as your own.
- Deliver by the due date you promised. If something gets in the way, pause your listings before you fall behind.
- For live calls, show up on time. If you have to cancel, the customer gets a full refund.
- Be civil to other readers and to Hexxsy staff, on the site and off.

## If the code is broken

Most slips get a conversation, not a penalty. Serious ones don't.

| Step | When | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warning | A first, minor slip, like a late delivery or a pushy line in a listing | Hexxsy writes to you, says what needs to change, and keeps a note |
| Listing paused | The same slip again, or a listing that breaks the code | The listing comes down until it's fixed. The customer may be refunded |
| Shop paused | Repeated problems, or a complaint under review | All your listings come down. Paid orders still need delivering or refunding |
| Removal | Fear selling, sexual contact with a customer, misusing a customer's data, or ignoring someone in crisis | Your shop closes. Customers with open orders are refunded |

You can always reply and give your side before a pause or removal, except when a customer's safety can't wait.

Hexxsy may update this code. You'll get notice by email at least 14 days before a change takes effect.
