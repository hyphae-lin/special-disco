# Welcome email (draft)

Sent by the MailPoet "Welcome new subscribers" automation, right after someone signs up from the homepage. Every word here is editable in MailPoet.

Bracketed items are settings, not fixed values. Fill them from the coupon block's settings, and change them here whenever the coupon changes (see Welcome offer in `docs/SPEC.md`).

- `[DISCOUNT]`: the coupon amount, e.g. $5
- `[MINIMUM]`: the coupon's minimum spend, $25 at launch
- `[DAYS]`: days until the code expires, 30 at launch

## Subject line

Pick one:

- You left your name at the gate
- Something for your first visit
- I kept a key aside for you
- You found the crossroads. Here's a little something.

**Preheader** (the gray line inboxes show after the subject): A gift for your first visit to Hexxsy.
